Niacinamide is one of the most studied ingredients in modern skincare — and one of the most forgiving.

## What it does

- Supports ceramide production, reinforcing the skin barrier
- Reduces visible redness and post-acne marks
- Helps regulate sebum without stripping

## How to use it

Most people tolerate **2–5%** concentrations daily, morning or night. Pair it with a moisturizer and SPF in the AM.

> Pro tip: there's no need to chase 10%+ formulas. The research sweet spot sits comfortably below that.
