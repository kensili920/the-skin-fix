Retinol is the gold standard for cell turnover, but starting wrong is the fastest way to quit.

## The starter framework

1. **Twice a week**, at night, for the first two weeks.
2. Apply on **dry** skin after cleansing.
3. Follow with a bland moisturizer (ceramides love this job).
4. **Always** SPF the next morning.

After a month, work up to every other night. Most skin types never need more than that.

## Signs you're going too fast

- Stinging that lasts more than a minute
- Flaking that doesn't respond to moisturizer
- New breakouts beyond the initial "purge" weeks

Pull back to once a week and rebuild slowly.
